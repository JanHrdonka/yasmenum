Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short
  • Yasmenum collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Everything you create in the app stays on your device.
  • We never receive, see or sell your data.

This policy covers the Yasmenum app for iPhone, iPad and Android. Yasmenum asks a short question before an app you chose to limit opens.

Who is responsible

Operator: details will be added before the app is released. Contact: see Contact below.

What stays on your device

The app stores the following only on your device, in its own private storage:

  • the apps you chose to protect (their name and system identifier; on iPhone also an icon picture),
  • question sets, including the ones you create or import,
  • your answers and the statistics made from them (for example how often you paused before an app),
  • your settings and whether you bought Yasmenum Pro.

The Android app also keeps a short event log for the Diagnostics screen. It stays on the phone unless you share it yourself.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

How the app notices that a protected app opens

  • iPhone and iPad: Yasmenum uses Apple's Screen Time framework or a Shortcuts automation that you set up yourself. With Screen Time, Apple gives the app only coded tokens for the apps you pick instead of their names, and no information about how you use them. Everything runs on the device.
  • Android: Yasmenum uses an Accessibility service that you turn on yourself after an explanation in the app. It reads only the package name of the app that comes to the foreground, and compares it on the phone with your list of protected apps. It does not read screen content, what you type, or notifications. The Android app doesn't connect to the internet itself; purchases go through the Google Play app.

Services from Apple and Google

  • App icons (iPhone and iPad only): to show the real icon of an app you protect, Yasmenum asks Apple's public App Store search service for it. The request contains only that app's identifier or name and your App Store region. Like any internet request, it reaches Apple with your device's IP address. We receive nothing. If the request fails, the app shows a letter instead.
  • Purchases: Yasmenum Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment. We never receive your payment details or your name. The app only learns whether Pro has been bought.
  • Ratings: the app may show the system rating prompt. It is provided by Apple or Google.

These services are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Things you share yourself

  • Question packs: you can export a question set as a file and send it with the share sheet. It goes only where you send it.
  • Creating questions with AI: the app can copy a prompt for you to paste into an AI assistant of your choice. Yasmenum sends nothing to any AI service. What you paste there is covered by that service's own privacy policy.

Notifications

Reminders and summaries are created on the device. Yasmenum doesn't use push notifications or a server.

Backups

On iPhone and iPad, app data can be included in your own iCloud or computer backup, depending on your device settings. That backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by us. The Android app turns system backup off.

Keeping and deleting data

Data stays on your device until you delete it. In Settings you can delete all statistics (Delete All Statistics), and you can delete question sets and protected apps one by one. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.

Your rights

Because we don't collect or hold any personal data, there is nothing we could give you, correct or delete; you control all of it in the app. You can still contact us with any question. If you live in the EU, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In the Czech Republic this is the Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOU).

Children

Yasmenum isn't directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before the new version is released.

Contact

Contact details will be added before the app is released.
