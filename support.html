Common questions

The question doesn't appear when I open a protected app

  • iPhone and iPad: open Yasmenum → Apps. An app marked "Setup needed" isn't connected yet; follow the guide in the app.
  • Android: open Yasmenum and tap "Set Up" on the "App protection is off" banner. Check that the Accessibility service is on and that battery optimization doesn't stop Yasmenum (Huawei and Samsung phones need this).

How do I get Pro on a new device?

Settings → Yasmenum Pro → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

How do I move my question sets to another device?

Open a set and use Share to export it as a file, then open the file on the other device with Yasmenum. Packs work between iPhone and Android.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is on your device. Settings → Delete All Statistics removes your history; deleting the app removes everything. See the Privacy Policy.
